04 / behind the work

Two hands.
Two ways of seeing.

AEMI began as a shared idea, and now it’s becoming a home for the art we love making. Yua paints; CJ draws. Our styles are different, but we both believe a piece of art can hold a moment, a memory, or a feeling that’s hard to explain.

This space will grow with us — one finished piece, one experiment, and one story at a time.
