OUR STORY

AEMI started with two people, one recipe, and the idea of creating something of our own. Our first batch was small, but it gave us a glimpse of what AEMI could become. People tried it, enjoyed it, and supported our little start. But we also learned that making something good was only the beginning. We needed to do more than just make the product—we needed to build the brand behind it. So, we started again. AEMI Round 2 is our second try, this time with more planning, better systems, and a clearer vision of where we want to take it. Still small. Still ours. But this time, we're building it with intention.
